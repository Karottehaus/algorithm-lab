# DICOM Data Exploration Options

| Direction                        | What to explore                                                                | Suitable scenarios                                                         | Example output                                                    |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Series and metadata inspection   | Slice count, spacing, orientation, acquisition and reconstruction settings     | Checking whether a volume is ready for analysis; comparing scan settings   | Series summary and geometry report                                |
| Orthogonal and oblique slices    | Internal structures in three perpendicular planes or an arbitrary plane        | Finding small features; following an inclined crack or elongated structure | Slice viewer and annotated cross-sections                         |
| Windowing and local inspection   | Different intensity display ranges and cropped regions                         | Revealing details hidden by the full intensity range                       | Side-by-side views of the same ROI                                |
| Intensity distributions          | Histograms, percentiles, regional statistics, and profiles across boundaries   | Comparing regions; choosing initial segmentation thresholds                | Histograms and intensity profiles                                 |
| Slice-by-slice statistics        | Mean intensity, variation, foreground area, and centroid along the stack       | Locating abrupt changes, unusual slices, or changes in cross-section       | Curves linked to selected slices                                  |
| Projection images                | Maximum, minimum, or mean intensity along a viewing direction                  | Summarizing bright, dark, or overall structures                            | Projection images; inspect slices to resolve overlapping features |
| Volume rendering                 | How internal structures appear under different color and opacity settings      | Understanding spatial relationships and presenting the scan                | Interactive 3D view                                               |
| Segmentation                     | Separate a target using thresholds, region growing, or manual labels           | Isolating an object, anatomical structure, pore, or defect                 | Binary or labeled 3D mask                                         |
| Connected components             | Number, size, and position of separate segmented objects                       | Counting particles, pores, or disconnected regions                         | Component labels and size distribution                            |
| Surface reconstruction           | Extract a boundary mesh from a mask or intensity level                         | Inspecting shape; exporting geometry for further work                      | Surface mesh and cross-sections                                   |
| Shape and size measurements      | Volume, surface area, principal axes, elongation, and compactness              | Comparing objects, particles, or segmented structures                      | Measurement table and shape comparisons                           |
| Local thickness and distance     | Distance to boundaries and a defined local thickness measure                   | Studying walls, struts, layers, or internal cavities                       | Distance map and thickness distribution                           |
| Skeleton and network analysis    | Centerlines, branches, paths, and endpoints                                    | Studying tubular structures, fibers, or pore networks                      | Skeleton overlay and network statistics                           |
| Connectivity and porosity        | Void fraction, isolated versus connected voids, and boundary-to-boundary paths | Industrial CT of porous materials and internal defects                     | Porosity estimate and connectivity map                            |
| Texture and heterogeneity        | Local variance, entropy, and directional texture                               | Comparing uniformity or spatial patterns within matched ROIs               | Texture maps and regional feature table                           |
| Noise and artifact inspection    | Fluctuations in uniform regions, rings, streaks, and discontinuities           | Assessing image quality before segmentation                                | Quality observations and selected examples                        |
| Denoising experiments            | Compare smoothing or edge-preserving filters with the original scan            | Testing whether noise reduction improves a downstream task                 | Matched views and changes in measured features                    |
| Registration and change analysis | Align multiple scans and compare corresponding regions                         | Studying loading, aging, treatment, or repeated acquisition                | Aligned overlays and change maps                                  |
| Sensitivity and repeatability    | Vary thresholds, preprocessing, ROI boundaries, and resolution                 | Determining how stable a reported measurement is                           | Parameter sweep and measurement range                             |
